About the numbers

  • Odds are the best price found across tracked bookmakers, refreshed once a day. Your own bookmaker's price may differ — always confirm before staking.
  • Win chance for each leg is implied from the odds, adjusted so all outcomes for a game sum to 100%.
  • Injury and head-to-head notes are pulled once a day per game and are a summary, not a guarantee — always check for last-minute changes.
  • Hit chance multiplies each leg's chance together. Every extra leg lowers it.
  • No slip is guaranteed. Only stake what you can afford to lose.
